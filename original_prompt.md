**Extracted Rules:**
1. Provide a unified numbered list of grammar errors and typos.
2. Ignore stylistic issues.
3. Ignore unapplied sandhi (including visarga sandhi and mo'anusvarah).
4. Ignore hyphens in samasas, but ensure any applied sandhi within samasas is correct.
5. Do not ignore wrongly applied sandhi.
6. Enforce past tense strictly using the *kta* suffix instead of *ktavatu*, ensuring the correct voice is used (active for intransitive verbs, passive for transitive verbs). This strict enforcement applies to:
   - *Ktavatu* participles (`-वान्`, `-वती`, `-वन्तः`, `-वत्यः`).
   - All finite past tense verbs: *Laṅ* (Imperfect) and *Liṭ* (Perfect) lakāras.
   - Past continuous constructions using *sma* (`स्म`) or a present participle with a past copula.
   - *Note: Conditional mood (*Lṛṅ lakāra*) must be preserved and should not be replaced with *kta*.*
7. For spans containing only spoken dialogue in quotes (without speaker info within the span itself), prefix the quote with the speaker's name followed by a hyphen.
